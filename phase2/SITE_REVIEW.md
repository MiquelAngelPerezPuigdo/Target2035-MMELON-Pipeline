## Clarifications affecting our workflow

| Topic | Official information and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Ranking | Chemical series recovered among the selected 50 comes first, then hit count, full-library PR AUC, and ROC AUC. Optimize a useful portfolio of likely hits across chemistry, rather than only global AUC. [Evaluation](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/wiki/641047). |
| Series labels | Official series were built from all assay hits using MCS frequency against the full approximately 460K screened library. They cannot be reconstructed from the released labels alone. ECFP/Murcko groups are local proxies. [Series method](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=15113), [library denominator](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=15181). |
| Inhibitor counts | The organizer confirms that the inhibitor column is censored to compounds observed in the PGK2 arm. A comparable inhibitor-only supplement will not be supplied; the previously mentioned data came from a different replicate. Do not interpret the reported subtotal as sequencing depth. [Confirmation](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=15177). |
| Control supplement | Questions about repeated rows, overlapping structures, and aggregation remain unanswered in the reviewed NTC thread. Keep the conservative presence-based use; do not add overlapping counts blindly. [NTC discussion](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=14860). |
| Normalized Z | The provider describes the Faver normalized statistic using naive and selected read proportions. Cross-library performance has not been independently validated, and naive reads are unavailable. Treat its interpretation separately from a conventional depth-dependent Z score. [Z discussion](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=14425), [naive reads](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=14426). |
| Validation feedback | Organizers allow reuse of validation data and evaluation feedback, provided its use is declared in the writeup. Our forced labels and adaptive bags must be disclosed and kept separate from independent evaluation. [Clarification](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=15354). |
| Eligibility | The clarified better-than-random threshold is unadjusted p < 0.01 in either retrospective step. Either of a team's two submissions can qualify; order is irrelevant. The two-series statement concerned the blind step, so do not assume the same numeric threshold after changing the eligible universe. A proper writeup and significant DEL contribution are still required. [Eligibility clarification](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=15248), [best submission](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn75349604/discussion/threadId=14549). |

## First experiment sequence

1. **Diagnose transfer before fitting.** Score the 6,580 released structures with the frozen DEL ensemble and historical ligand/count rankings. For each of the 39 binders, record rank, DEL support, similarity to public ligands, and nearby labeled negatives. Measure top-cutoff enrichment and both PR metrics on this participant-selected sample; do not treat these as performance estimates for the remaining library.
2. **Freeze grouped comparisons.** Keep duplicate parents together and group close analogs, not just exact Murcko scaffolds. With only 39 positives, record the positive count in every held-out group and use a small predefined model set. Keep historical adaptive validation evidence separate from the release holdouts.
3. **Compare substantial DEL adaptation.** Begin with frozen DEL predictions, a regularized residual/adaptation model using DEL scores and molecular features, and a labels-only diagnostic comparator. Use a DEL-removal ablation, a structural holdout, and the eight-row sensitivity analysis to assess whether DEL contributes meaningfully. If it does not, revisit DEL proxy labels and representation rather than adding a nominal DEL weight.
4. **Pause validation selection after repeated zero scores.** The three proposed lists were submitted and all returned zero hits, following three earlier zero-hit validation results. Do not submit another list from these rankings.
